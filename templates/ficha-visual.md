# Visual profile

Made for the people who will use the product, not for the builder's taste. This guides the work; the owner's eye and the customer's eye have the last word.

## Palette
Few colors, each with a job (identity, data, status). Neutrals for everything else.

## Typography
One common, legible family. Sizes that fit dense information.

## Density
How much information per screen, and how real the sample data looks.

## Shape
Corner radius, borders, shadows.

## Reference qualities
Qualities taken from references the owner approved (never a copy of someone's design). One line each.

## Banned (AI look)
- purple-blue gradients
- frosted glass and blur effects
- oversized rounded cards
- glow effects and neon outlines
- emoji used as icons
- round placeholder numbers (1000, 500, 50%) instead of believable data
